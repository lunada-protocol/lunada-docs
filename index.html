What it is

One asset, two sides of the same chain.

LUNADA is one program on Solana. In the open, an asset is an ordinary Solana token in an ordinary account. Inside the pool it is a private note, and the chain stores only its hash. A holding crosses from one side to the other in one transaction.

Private by default

A transfer hides the asset, the amount, the sender and the receiver. No operator, committee or outside network sees them.

Standard tokens

LUNADA defines no token format of its own. A token is created and minted by Token-2022, Solana’s current token program, and its issuer keeps the mint authority.

Rules per token

The auditor and the list of approved holders belong to one token. They are fixed when it is created, and every holder can read them before accepting the asset.

Proof in place of trust

A transfer that breaks a rule has no valid proof and cannot take place. No intermediary approves a single transfer.

What the pool holds

SOL, and new tokens that carry at least one rule.

A token enters the pool only if its issuer created it with an auditor, a list of approved holders, or both. Tokens that already exist, and tokens with neither rule, stay outside.

For issuers

Set the rules once, when the token is created.

The issuer works in the LUNADA wallet, a browser extension. Its Solana account is the issuer of the token and stays its mint authority.

Create the token

Choose the symbol and the two rules. For an audited token, name the audit key: your own, or the key of a registrar, a transfer agent or an audit firm. Creating the token and registering it with the pool are one transaction, so the rules hold from the first unit.

  • Issue and redeem. The issuer mints new units straight into a private note, its own or an investor’s. The public sees that the supply grew, and not who received the units. A burn lowers the public supply by the same amount.
  • The public side. With the option “public accounts need approval”, units leave the pool only to Solana accounts the issuer has approved, for example a custodian’s. The wallet states the price first: an issuer with this power can freeze any public account of the token, the pool’s vault included.
  • What an issuer cannot do. Freeze, move or take back a private note. Its controls are the mint authority, the auditor’s read access, the member list and, where chosen, the approval of public accounts.

For auditors

Read every private movement of one token. Change nothing.

An auditor gives its audit key to the issuer. Once a token is created with it, every private movement of that token carries one more encrypted copy for the auditor, and the proof shows that the copy is correct. A movement without it has no valid proof.

Read only

For good

A token has one audit key, and it cannot be changed or removed. An issuer chooses it with the same care as the custody of the asset itself.

For holders

Show a bank one fact, and nothing else.

A holder answers a bank, a lender or a buyer with a short text that the reader checks alone against Solana. It can open one note, prove one payment, or hand over a view key that reads a whole account and cannot spend. It can also prove a minimum balance without opening anything.

Relayers and fees

Anyone can run a relayer. Nobody has to use one.

A Solana transaction needs a fee payer, and a fee payer is a public account. A relayer signs as fee payer and nothing else. In the same transaction the program repays it in ordinary SOL out of the sender’s private SOL. It cannot take its fee without the action taking place, and it never sees a key or an opened note.

Open to anyone

Relayers are permissionless. Anyone can run one, and the protocol itself takes no fee.

Its own price

A relayer charges what it chooses to cover the cost of its service, for example twice the network fee. The wallet shows the price before the holder confirms.

A public register

Relayers register on this page. The wallet picks one from the register, picks one at random, or uses a relayer the holder names.

Never required

A holder can always pay the network fee from the wallet’s own Solana account, in the open, and use no relayer at all.

How it is built

One program and its proofs. No operator, no committee, no second chain.

FoundationsBen-Sasson et al., Zerocash, IEEE S&P 2014. Groth, On the Size of Pairing-Based Non-interactive Arguments, EUROCRYPT 2016. Grassi et al., Poseidon, USENIX Security 2021. Bowe, Gabizon and Miers, Scalable Multi-party Computation for zk-SNARK Parameters, 2017. The white paper gives the full design, the limits and the measurements.

Notes
The program keeps a tree of note commitments. The chain never stores a note, only a hash that binds its asset, amount and owner. Spending a note publishes a one-time mark, the nullifier, so no note is spent twice. The construction follows Zerocash.
Proofs
Each private action carries a Groth16 zero-knowledge proof of 256 bytes on the BN254 curve, which the program verifies with Solana’s built-in curve operations. Proofs are made in the wallet, so no secret leaves the holder’s machine.
Settlement
Every action settles in one Solana transaction.
Keys
The proving keys will come from a public ceremony with many independent participants. One honest participant is enough to make forgery impossible.

Status and roadmap

Built. Not yet on a public network.

The Solana program with the private pool, the disclosures, audited and members-only tokens, the browser wallet, a relayer and an event recorder are implemented and run in a test environment.

  1. December 2026

    Larger transfers and wallet hardening

    Up to eight notes in and out, a sender memo for restored wallets, proof of holdings for audited and members-only tokens, deposits through a relayer.

  2. January 2027

    Public test network

    Solana devnet with several independent relayers and a public event recorder. Anyone can try the wallet with test tokens.

  3. February 2027

    Full independent audit

    The circuits and the Solana program, with the report published.

  4. February 2027

    Public key ceremony

    For the proving keys of every circuit, held after the audit. It is public, and backers are welcome to take part.

  5. March 2027

    Mainnet launch

    After the ceremony and the audit, with the upgrade key locked or under published governance.

  6. Q2 2027

    More assets

    NFTs, tokens that exist once, with private holding and a public side, and private sales of a note in one transaction.

A first external audit has taken place and its fixes are applied. A full independent audit before mainnet is pending. The proving keys are development keys, so the system must not hold real value before that audit and the public key ceremony.

Who it is for

Issuers of tokenised securities, fund units and real-world assets on Solana, and the institutions that hold and trade them. A professional marketing and growth team will be employed to address them.

Why now

Tokenised real-world assets on public chains grew from about USD 12 billion to about USD 32 billion in the year to June 2026 (source: RWA.xyz), and issuers are choosing their chain now. Solana checks zero-knowledge proofs on chain at low cost.
